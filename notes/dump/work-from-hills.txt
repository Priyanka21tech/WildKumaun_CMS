## WILD KUMAON- An Eco-Resort at Sattal
It is one of the purely dedicated Wildlife Lodges in Uttarakhand, Himalayas, India. The team is dedicated to hosting a guest in ethical ways and eco-friendly practices. This place to stay is preferred by Nature Lovers and guests who want to accommodate themselves in a fresh environment and only between a few people. Accommodation is perfect if you are visiting Sattal to enjoy its birds and Nature. Wild Kumaon also loves to host on monthly basis, long stays in a Homestay manner for Work from Hills/Work from Home. Accommodation is surrounded by Naturally grown forest and is connected to the Reserve forest of Sattal. The Premises has Oak, Bay Berry and Rhododendron. The location provides good access to the market and necessary things in an emergency but also to the Wildlife, Nature and Birds of Sattal on another side. Located inside the Sattal territory. This is a wonderful place to accommodate yourself when you are visiting Sattal.
