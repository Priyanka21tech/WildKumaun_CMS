BIRDING, KUMAONI CUISINE & A SMALL CHOCOLATE FACTORY IN THE MOUNTAINS
One of the most popular hill stations in Uttarakhand is Nainital. And this famous getaway is also called the LAKE DISTRICT OF INDIA. That’s because over and above Nainital aur Bhimtal, the region is dotted with a bunch of lakes – some known and some unknown ones. And one such gem is Sattal, which is actually a cluster of seven freshwater lakes. These pristine mountain lakes nestled within dense forest is definitely one of the most stunning places of Uttarakhand.
In this episode, we’ll go for a morning bird walk, have a Kumaoni lunch and end the day at a very special chocolate factory in the Himalayas.
