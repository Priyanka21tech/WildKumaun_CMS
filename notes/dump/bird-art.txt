## Paintings by Abha Singh
What Abha says about her Art,
Painting is a way of life for me. It’s a combination of old and new methods. It’s constantly evolving.
Although I paint diverse subjects, birds are special for me. They rouse my imagination and creativity. It gives me the joy to paint a bird that I spot in the wild. Having studied great artists like Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Raja Ravi Verma, Kishangarh art, etc during my Masters in Fine Arts, I enjoy the conservative style of art for instance good old impressionist art or abstract art. At the same time, I enjoy experimenting with new concepts like pour in acrylic.
