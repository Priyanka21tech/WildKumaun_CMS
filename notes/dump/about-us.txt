## ABOUT US
‘WILD KUMAON’ was founded by like-minded entrepreneurs, people who share a passion for travel and exploring. Service, design and simplicity are at our core; when you book a room with us, you’re guaranteed to have an extraordinary experience. From the moment you walk in the door, you’ll feel the special Wild Kumaon hospitality.
Located in Sattal, a place between the Natural Oak forest, where a team exist, which hosts the guest and who are dedicated for providing outdoor experiences, Products, Hospitality and travel services in Sattal-Nainital and Kumaon region of Uttarakhand Himalayas.
Wild Kumaon also loves to host on monthly basis, long stays or in a Home stay manner for Work from Hills/Work from Home. Accommodation is surrounded by Naturally grown forest and is connected to Reserve forest of Sattal. The Premises has Oak, Bay Berry and Rhododendron. The location provides good access to market and necessary things in an emergency but also to the Wildlife, Nature and Birds of Sattal on other side. Located inside the Sattal territory..this is a wonderful place to accommodate yourself when you are visiting Sattal, Nainital, Bhimtal, Naukuchiyataal, Ghodakhal, Mukteshwar, Almora, Ranikhet, Pangot. At the Heart of Sattal… Wild Kumaon and its team is always ready to host you and Provide you homely experiences, Birding tours, Bird Guides and Adventure in Sattal. The property and team specialize in hosting Birders or Bird watchers from all over the Globe. Campus has Kitchen garden and a Bird Feeder or Bird Hide also. Remote or Dome Tent, Camping in Sattal can also be provided. property has one Dining place cum Workshop Hall also. Wild Kumaon look forward to host you and create beautiful memories and reasons to visits Sattal again.
